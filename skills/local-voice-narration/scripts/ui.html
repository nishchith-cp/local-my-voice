Local voice narration

Try a short sample in your authorized voice, listen, and export. Then replace the sample with your own transcript and generate another take.

1. Know what happens

Setup installs Python dependencies into an isolated environment. The first online generation downloads Qwen3-TTS model files from Hugging Face (mlx-community/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-0.6B-Base-8bit). Disk space and Apple Silicon unified memory are needed; download size and time vary.

Model files normally live in ~/.cache/huggingface/hub (unless your environment overrides it) and are reused. Cached-only mode is the default. Missing model files produce an error, not an automatic online retry.

Selecting a recording sends it only to this loopback service on your computer, not a cloud voice provider. The service converts it to mono 24 kHz, loads the model, generates speech locally, optionally filters noise, then encodes MP3. Model downloads can make external requests; optional telemetry is disabled where supported. This is not a guarantee of zero dependency networking.

Your authorized reference is saved privately on this computer in ~/Library/Application Support/local-voice-narration/authorized-reference.wav. It is reused for later transcripts and after restarting this service, until you replace or delete it. Permission is confirmed once per saved voice. Generated session audio remains temporary: download outputs before stopping the server. Nothing is added to the skill repository.
