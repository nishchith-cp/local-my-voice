The thing about Luffy is that he lives so completely in the moment. Of all the characters in any anime, he’s the one who isn’t tortured by his past. He has a painful past, but he isn’t tormented by it. And he has a goal for the future, but he isn’t so fixated on that goal that he loses sight of what’s right in front of him. He’s always living fully in the moment. Sometimes, that makes him hilarious, because he does exactly what he wants to do right then. And sometimes, it means he’s the only one who sees clearly enough, and has his priorities straight enough, to do what needs to be done.
